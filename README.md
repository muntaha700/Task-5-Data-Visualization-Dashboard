# Task-5-Data-Visualization-Dashboard
1. Bar Chart – Clients by Service
Insight: This chart shows the number of clients using each service offered by CoreTech.
It helps identify the most popular services and areas generating the highest demand.
Management can use this information for resource allocation and marketing strategies.

2. Pie Chart – Lead Source Distribution
Insight: The pie chart illustrates how clients discovered CoreTech.
It highlights the most effective lead generation channels.
Marketing efforts can be optimized by focusing on the highest-performing sources.

3. Line Chart – Monthly Client Trend
Insight: This chart tracks client acquisition trends over time.
Peaks indicate periods of higher customer engagement or successful campaigns.
It helps in forecasting future growth and planning business activities.

5. Histogram – Ratings Distribution
Insight: The histogram displays the distribution of customer ratings.
It helps assess overall customer satisfaction levels.
A concentration of higher ratings indicates positive service performance.
